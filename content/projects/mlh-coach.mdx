## Overview

Since November 2026 I have coached for Major League Hacking on the event floor. At HackUTA at UT Arlington, I mentored 100+ hackers from first idea to final submission.

## What I do

- **Mentoring and debugging** — work side by side with teams to scope ideas, debug across their stacks, and ship working demos before the submission deadline.
- **MLH table and sponsor challenges** — run the MLH table and the sponsor challenge tracks (Solana, Tiger Data, DEV), guiding teams on prize requirements and integrations.
- **Workshops and mini-events** — lead hands-on sessions that give beginners a low-pressure way into building.
- **Judging and prize submissions** — support judging and make sure teams meet MLH challenge criteria and submit on time.
- **Onboarding first-time hackers** — walk newcomers through team formation, tooling setup, and their first project pitch.

## Takeaway

Coaching shows me the other side of a hackathon. The win that matters is a team shipping something they are proud of, and a good mentor makes that more likely.

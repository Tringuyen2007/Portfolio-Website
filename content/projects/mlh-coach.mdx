## Overview

In November 2026 I joined Major League Hacking as a coach, and the role is ongoing. At HackUTA at UT Arlington, I mentored 100+ hackers from first idea to final submission.

## What I do

- **Mentoring and debugging** — I work alongside hackers on their projects, helping them get unstuck and keep building.
- **Running the MLH table** — I staff the table and the sponsor challenges, including Solana, Tiger Data, and DEV.
- **Workshops and mini-events** — I lead sessions that give hackers new skills and a break from the grind.
- **Judging and prizes** — I support the judging process and help teams with prize submissions.
- **Onboarding first-timers** — I help first-time hackers get started with confidence.

## Takeaway

Coaching shows me the other side of a hackathon: the win that matters is a team shipping something they are proud of, and a good mentor makes that more likely.

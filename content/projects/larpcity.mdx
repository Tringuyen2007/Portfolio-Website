## Overview

LarpCity teaches people to make better financial decisions by letting them live out how those decisions play out. It won the **Capital One track at HackRice**.

Every player starts at 22, just out of college, with a randomized salary, student debt, and a 600 credit score. From there, time advances day by day through a living isometric city. Paychecks land, bills arrive, the market crashes and recovers, cars break down — and the player decides how to respond, all the way to retirement.

## What it does

- **Seeded simulation engine** modeling debt, credit, investments, taxes, income, expenses, and major life events. Runs replay exactly, so the end-of-game review can rewind to any past decision and show what would have worked on the same market.
- **Real-world financial data** — a real card catalog from CFPB plans and FRED rates stored in Tiger Data, and a year of real market data.
- **AI coaching** with Gemini that analyzes decisions at goals, bankruptcies, and big portfolio swings, and explains long-term outcomes.
- **A phone in the city** that acts as the hub: Stocks, Goals, Map, Calendar, News, Mail, Bank, and Taxes.

## Stack

TypeScript, Vite, and PixiJS for the game; an Express API server over Tiger Data (Postgres with TimescaleDB); Gemini for coaching and the in-game newspaper; deployed on Vultr.

## Takeaway

A deterministic, seeded engine was the best decision we made — it gave us exact rewinds, fast-forwards, and reproducible demos for free, and made the "what went wrong" review possible.

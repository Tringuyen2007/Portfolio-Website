## Overview

Mira is an at-home physical therapy coach that turns a webcam into a live 3D model of the patient. It won **1st Overall** and **MLH Best Use of Tiger Data** at CodeRED Orion.

The system tracks reps and form in real time, checks in when it detects a fall, and gives therapists a dashboard and an AI assistant grounded in their patient's actual session data. It spans a vision engine, an iPhone and Apple Watch app, a therapist dashboard, and a kiosk display, all connected through one server.

## What I built

I owned the server — the piece every other part of Mira plugs into.

- **Real-time backend** in FastAPI with WebSocket streams for the device and dashboard, streaming 30 Hz pose data from the vision engine.
- **Time-series storage** on TimescaleDB (Tiger Data), reaching **41× faster queries** and **2.74× lossless compression** on pose data.
- **Four AI agents** (three using Gemini) for live coaching, fall alerts, session insights, and therapist Q&A, each grounded in real patient data.
- **Safety path** — fall check-in and emergency call handling — plus the REST API the iOS app and dashboard depend on.

## Takeaway

A 30 Hz stream of pose frames adds up fast. Choosing the right storage early — and designing the server contract so four teammates could build their pieces in parallel — is what let us ship a working end-to-end system in a hackathon weekend.

# Redesign research notes (2026-11-02)

## Current site audit
- Palette: neutral charcoal (#14161a) with gray accent; no hue → reads "default dark mode", not "night city".
- Type: Fraunces (serif display) + Geist. Pleasant but generic.
- Home: hero + 2 text-only project previews. Little visual weight on the actual wins.
- Projects: 3D ring carousel. Only ~3 of 10 cards readable at once, awards hidden, needs drag.
- Backgrounds: static star field only. No sense of place.

## Direction chosen: "Night City" (rooftop, editorial, cinematic)
- Base: ink-navy sky, skyline silhouette with lit windows, haze/bloom, few stars.
- Accents: warm sodium-amber primary, cool ice-blue secondary (links/live states).
- Motif: small mono labels (01–10, years, coordinates) like signage.
- Avoid: magenta/green neon soup, glitch, scanlines (cyberpunk cliché).

## Projects section replacement
- Featured block for the 4 award winners (Mira, Radar, LarpCity, Vision).
- Indexed list 01–10 below: title, summary, year, award badge, stack, hover preview.
- Everything visible at once; no drag; stacks cleanly on mobile.
